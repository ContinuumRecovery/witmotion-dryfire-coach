Camera setup

Where should the camera be?

Place your phone on a tripod or stand off to the side of the target line — between you and the target, looking sideways at the target. It does not need to be straight-on.

  • BestPhone on a tripod off to one side, 3–6 feet from the target, lens pointed AT the target. Tilt up to 30° off perpendicular is fine — the app corrects perspective.
  • BestThe whole printed sheet fills at least half the camera view. The 4 corner markers must all be visible.
  • OKCamera farther back is OK if markers stay sharp — zoom out, never in. Pinch-zoom blurs the markers.
  • AvoidDon’t put the phone behind you aimed downrange — muzzle flash and laser glare will overwhelm the lens. Side angle is safer and works better.
  • AvoidGlossy paper + a bright overhead light = washed-out markers. Use matte paper, side or ambient lighting.

Pick a calibration mode

Three ways to lock the target. Pick one — you can change later.

Tip: if ArUco shows “searching…” for more than a few seconds, the markers are too small in the frame — move the phone closer or zoom out.

Set your aim point, then dry-fire

Once the target locks (the badge in the corner says “ArUco lock” or “locked”):

  • 1Tap Set aim point, then tap where on the target you’re aiming (usually the bullseye).
  • 2Tap Start session. The app turns on the camera (for laser tracking) and the mic (to detect the click).
  • 3With your laser bore‑sighter in the chamber, take a dry shot. The mic catches the click; the camera measures where the laser landed.
  • ViewTap Rectified (top‑right of the stage) to see a flat top‑down view of the target with your hits.
  • TipHit Test shot any time to verify the pipeline without firing.
